A small program that writes one image summing up 21 November 2026 world news to `/output/entry.png`. The front page covers the day's eight biggest stories: the historic Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral summit in Shenzhen, Russian missiles hitting Poland and NATO's grave-violation response, the S&P 500 and Dow entering correction as AI stocks plunge, the ICJ ruling that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, new UK–EU deals on food trade, carbon markets and youth mobility, the Saudi coalition intercepting Houthi missiles over Riyadh and striking Hodeidah, the Iran war nearing its ninth month, and NASA confirming Voyager 1 has passed one light-day from Earth. The image itself ships in this repo as `entry.jpg`; the script simply places an exact copy where the runner expects it, so what you see here is exactly what gets judged.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
